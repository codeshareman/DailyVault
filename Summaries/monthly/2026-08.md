### 条目分类

```dataview
TABLE length(rows) AS 数量
FROM "Daily"
FLATTEN file.lists AS item
FLATTEN item.tags AS tag
WHERE month = this.month AND startswith(tag, "#kind/")
GROUP BY tag
SORT length(rows) DESC
```
